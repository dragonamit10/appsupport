Cook Money

Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

Data collection

Cook Money does not collect, transmit to the developer, sell, rent, or share personal information, financial information, usage information, identifiers, diagnostics, or location data.

The app does not use advertising trackers, analytics services, or a developer-operated account or server.

Data stored on your devices and in iCloud

Information you enter is stored for the app on your device. When iCloud synchronization is available and enabled, the information may also be stored and synchronized by Apple through your personal iCloud account so it can appear on your supported Apple devices.

The developer does not receive or have access to your Cook Money records stored on your devices or in your iCloud account. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's terms and privacy policies.

Deletion and retention

Cook Money does not automatically delete your records. Records are removed only when you choose to delete specific information or use an available monthly, yearly, or app reset option. Data managed by iCloud may synchronize those user-requested changes across your devices.

Purchases

App Store purchases, purchase restoration, refunds, and related transaction information are handled by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment-card or banking information.

Children's privacy

Because the developer does not collect personal information through Cook Money, the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change. The current version will remain available on this page with its effective date.
