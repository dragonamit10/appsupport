Shri Ram Shalaka Prashnavali

Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Data collection

Shri Ram Shalaka Prashnavali does not collect, transmit to the developer, sell, rent, or share personal information, usage information, identifiers, diagnostics, or location data.

The app does not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics services, or a developer-operated account or server.

Offline operation

The app's grid, chaupais, meanings, explanations, and standard touch controls operate locally on your device. The developer does not receive the question you think of, the letter you select, or the result displayed by the app.

Siri and voice features

If you voluntarily use Siri or another Apple speech service, Apple may process the interaction according to your device settings and Apple's privacy policies. The developer does not receive or store your voice recording, Siri request, or speech transcript. The app remains usable through its touch controls when Siri or speech services are unavailable.

Purchases

App Store purchases, refunds, and related transaction information are handled by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment-card or banking information.

Children's privacy

Because the developer does not collect personal information through the app, Shri Ram Shalaka Prashnavali does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or privacy practices change. The current version will remain available on this page with its effective date.
