Seekora

Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Data collection

Seekora does not collect, transmit to the developer, sell, rent, or share personal information, inventory information, photos, usage information, identifiers, diagnostics, or location data.

The app does not use advertising trackers, analytics services, or a developer-operated account or server.

Data stored on your device

Items, categories, locations, box numbers, descriptions, remarks, custom fields, and photos you choose are stored locally for the app on your device. The developer cannot access this information.

Seekora stores the free-trial start date securely in your device Keychain. This value is used only to determine trial access and is not sent to the developer.

Photos, imports, and exports

Photos are accessed only when you choose them for an inventory item. CSV and Excel files are read only when you select them for import. Exports are created only when you request them and are shared using system controls. These files and their contents are not uploaded to the developer.

Deletion and retention

Your inventory remains on your device until you edit or delete it, remove the app and its data, or otherwise use system data-management controls. Exported files remain wherever you choose to save or share them.

Purchases

The seven-day trial is managed locally. App Store purchases, restoration, refunds, and related transaction information are handled by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment-card or banking information.

Children's privacy

Because the developer does not collect personal information through Seekora, the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Seekora's features or privacy practices change. The current version will remain available here with its effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email dragon.amit@gmail.com.
