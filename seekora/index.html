Using Seekora

Create categories, add items with their storage location and box number, attach a photo, and record descriptions, remarks, or custom details.

Search across item names, locations, details, remarks, and photo-recognition keywords. You can also import CSV or Excel files and export a copy of your inventory.

Privacy at a glance

Seekora does not collect, track, sell, or share your personal information. Your inventory and selected photos are processed and stored locally by the app.

Trial and lifetime access

Seekora includes a seven-day free trial followed by an optional one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes purchases and restoration. Use Restore Purchase in the app while signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
