Privacy at a glance

The developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share your personal information or financial records.

Your Cook Money records remain under your control. If iCloud synchronization is enabled, Apple stores and synchronizes the app data in your personal iCloud account. The developer does not operate a server for this data and cannot access it.

Records are deleted only when you choose an available reset or deletion action in the app.

Purchases

Cook Money offers a one-time App Store purchase after its free access period. Purchases and restorations are processed by Apple. Use Restore Purchase in the app while signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
